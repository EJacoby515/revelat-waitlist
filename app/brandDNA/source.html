01The Idea

révélat / revealed / — only one letter is lit.

Every marketplace lists the thing. RÉVÉLAT reveals it. A seller puts a piece on, camera live; VisionAI recognises the worn item and opens it for bidding — closing the instant she moves on. No auctioneer. She performs; the room bids.

The identity carries one idea to the letter. In the wordmark, only the accented É is gold and lit — everything else obsidian and white. Gold never decorates; it marks the single thing being revealed for sale, exactly as the product is dark until worn and lit the moment it is recognised. The acute accent ´ is the ownable device: over the É, over the R, as the spark at the centre of the mark.

The gold É, spotlit against black-and-white, does the whole brand's work in a single glyph: the reveal, the accent device, and the rule that gold marks only what is for sale.

03Symbol · The Aperture
Symbol · viewBox 100×100

A six-blade iris opening onto a gold spark.

Rationale

One geometry, three readings — a camera lens (VisionAI, doing the recognising), a stage spotlight (the seller's performance), and a cut gemstone (the value being surfaced). The blades stay obsidian; only the spark at the centre is gold, holding the reveal rule. It resolves to a single lit dot at favicon scale and commands a billboard at sixteen feet.

04Monogram · The Accent R

A serif R, crowned by the gold acute.

Rationale

For seals, foil watermarks and merch where the full wordmark is too much, the monogram distils the brand to its device: the letterform in white or ink, the acute accent ´ in gold hovering above it — the same spark, relocated. It behaves like a maker's mark on the inside of a couture garment: quiet, certain, unmistakably RÉVÉLAT.

05Colour

Obsidian ground. Gold reveal. Purple atmosphere.

A dark-first system with radical discipline. The stage is black so the reveal can be the only warm light in the room. Purple lives in the air — glows and shadows — and never in text. Gold is identity. Red is action, and the two never meet.

The reserved-red rule. Gold is identity; red is action. Red (#FF3B5C / #FF1744) appears only on LIVE and BID — the live pulse, the bid button. It must never touch the wordmark, the monogram, or the aperture. This single restraint keeps the reveal sacred and the call-to-action unmistakable.

07Voice & Tone

Confident concierge. Name the moment, not the mechanism.

Short sentences, present tense, the product left to perform. We describe the reveal — never the plumbing. No hype, no discount language, no apologies. The room is already leaning in; the copy only has to point.

Do
“Bidding opens when she puts it on.”
“Revealed. Matched. Live.”
Exact numbers — “Current $340 · 12s left.”
Active verbs. Let the product perform.
Don't
No “!!!”, no shouting outside labels.
No “shop now / limited time / don't miss out.”
No naming the plumbing (“frame captured & classified”).
No apologies or hedging in errors.

08Motion

Motion marks the reveal — nothing else.

Animation is spent only where meaning happens: the item lighting up, a bid landing, a live channel breathing. Everywhere else the system holds still. Restraint is what makes the one lit moment read as the moment. All motion honours reduced-motion.

The Ignite

On reveal, the item fades from dark, scales 0.9 → 1, flares gold once, then settles to a steady glow. ~1.1s, ease (.2,.7,.2,1).

Live Pulse

The only red in motion — the live dot pulses #FF1744 every 2s. Bid counters roll in tabular mono; a new high flashes a gold hairline once.

Reveal Wipe

Items enter on a spotlight wipe — a radial gold light passing across the frame — as the aperture widens on the worn piece.
