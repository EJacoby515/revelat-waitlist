01Core

A live auction house where the reveal opens the bidding.

Sellers model clothing on camera. VisionAI matches the worn piece to their catalog in real time. Viewers bid while it's on — bidding opens when she puts it on, closes when she moves on. No auctioneer. The seller just performs. eBay meets Instagram Live — a fashion show where everything is for sale.

NameREVÉLAT — French révélé, "revealed." Say reh-vay-LAH.
One-linerShe puts it on. The room bids.
IdeaOf seven letters, only the accented É is gold. Gold marks only the item for sale — never decoration.
DeviceThe acute accent ´ — over the É, over the R, at the aperture's spark. No competitor owns it.
AudienceStyle-literate fashion resellers & buyers, 18+, live-commerce native.

03Voice & Tone

Confident concierge. Name the moment, not the mechanism.

Do

  • "Bidding opens when she puts it on."
  • "Revealed. Matched. Live."
  • Active verbs, exact numbers: "Current $340 · 12s left."
  • Present tense. Short sentences. Let the product perform.

Don't

  • No "!!!", no shouting outside labels.
  • No "shop now / limited time / don't miss out."
  • No naming the plumbing ("frame captured & classified").
  • No apologies or hedging in errors.

06Protecting the Mark

The gold accent is the whole idea. Four ways it breaks.

✕REVÉLAT
Never recolor the mark red — red is LIVE only.
✕REVÉLAT
Never drop the gold É. The accent must glow.
✕REVÉLAT
Never set the wordmark in a sans. Didone only.
✕REVÉLAT
Never crush the tracking. Keep it wide and lit.

Symbols: the wordmark can't live in an app icon. Two marks carry it — The Aperture (six-blade iris + gold spark = lens, spotlight, gem facet; recommended, app icon & favicon) and The Accent R (serif R under the gold accent; merch, seals, watermarks). Clearspace = the height of the E on all sides. Aperture holds to 16px; drop blade seams below 24px.

07Motion & UI Cues

The Ignite

Signature move. On reveal, the item (or the É) fades from dark, scales 0.9→1, flares gold once, then settles to a steady glow. ~1.1s, ease cubic-bezier(.2,.7,.2,1). Honor reduced-motion.

Live pulse

The only red motion. Live dot pulses #FF1744 every 2s. Bids: counters roll in mono tabular; new high flashes a gold border once.

Surfaces

Ground #0D0D0D with purple radial atmosphere. Card #1A1A1A, radius 14, hairline #2A2A2A. Radii: chip 4 / button 12 / card 14 / hero 20 / pill 999.

Gold discipline

Gold in UI is reserved for the revealed item and brand marks — never generic buttons. Bid CTA is red gradient #FF3B5C→#FF1744, gold-free.

Master Brief · paste block
Design for REVÉLAT — a live fashion auction app (eBay meets Instagram Live). Sellers model clothing on camera; VisionAI matches the worn item to their catalog in real time; viewers bid while it's on. Tone: luxury auction house × fashion magazine, built for live. Aesthetic: black stage, gold reveal, purple atmosphere. Core visual idea: "révélat" = revealed — only the accented É in the wordmark is gold; gold marks ONLY the thing for sale, never decoration. The acute accent (´) is the ownable device. Deliver a system, not one logo: (1) wordmark REVÉLAT in a high-contrast Didone serif, uppercase, wide tracking (+6%), the É gold (#D4AF37→#F5C842) with a soft spotlight glow; (2) app-icon symbol "The Aperture" — a six-blade camera iris opening onto a gold spark, reading as lens + spotlight + gem facet. Palette: obsidian #0D0D0D, gold #D4AF37 / #F5C842, purple #8B2FC9 / #1A0A2E, white #FFFFFF. Red #FF3B5C is RESERVED for LIVE/BID and must never touch the logo. Body type: neo-grotesque (Helvetica Neue / Inter). Data type: monospace. Style: Sotheby's × Vogue, cinematic dark, restrained, timeless — avoid neon, clutter, trends. Present it like a $500,000 brand project: mockups (iPhone app icon, black card with gold-foil É, authentication hang tag, billboard, live bidding overlay) plus a one-paragraph rationale per element and the positioning line "Every marketplace lists the thing.
